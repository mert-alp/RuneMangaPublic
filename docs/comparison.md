# RuneManga vs the other manga translators

**[← Back to the README](../README.md)** · **[All comparisons on runemanga.com](https://runemanga.com/compare)** · **[Website](https://runemanga.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=runemanga-public)**

If you are choosing a way to translate manga, manhwa or manhua, the first question is not *which tool*, it is **what you want back**: a quick idea of what one image says, a page re-lettered in your language, or a workbench where you correct every bubble yourself. The tools on this page answer different versions of that question.

> **How these facts were gathered.** Everything said about another tool comes from that tool's own pages, repository or store listings, as **checked on 2026-10-21**, and the sources are listed under each section. Products change, so confirm on the product's own page before you pay or install. Something out of date? Write to [support@runemanga.com](mailto:support@runemanga.com).

## Three kinds of tool

| Kind | Tools here | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Hosted manga translators** | RuneManga, Torii, Ichigo Reader | Upload pages or use an extension; the service does the work on its servers |
| **Open-source software you run yourself** | manga-image-translator, BallonsTranslator | Full control and offline options, in exchange for installing and running it |
| **General image translators** | Google Lens | Any image, from menus to signs; not built for comic pages |

## Torii alternative: RuneManga vs Torii Image Translator

Torii is the most complete hosted tool on this page today. According to its own pages it has a WebApp that takes images, ZIPs and PDFs and works in mobile browsers; an extension for Chrome (and Edge, Brave, Opera), Firefox on desktop and Android, and Safari on Mac, iPad and iPhone; more than 140 languages with source detection and vertical Japanese; AI inpainting and an editor for fonts, position and effects; an AI colorizer per page or per chapter; and a choice of AI models such as Gemini, GPT, Claude, DeepSeek and Grok, or your own API key. It is paid with credit packs that do not expire; the smallest listed pack is 2,500 credits for $5.99, and a page costs one scanning credit plus a translation cost that depends on the amount of text.

**RuneManga is narrower and simpler.** One button instead of an editor, a Free plan with 30 credits at sign-up and no card, a fixed short retention (about an hour), and one credit per page whatever the amount of text.

| Pick RuneManga when... | Pick Torii when... |
|---|---|
| You want to translate a chapter for free with a no-card account | You need Safari or Firefox for Android |
| You want pages deleted about an hour after the job | You want to edit the result: move text, change fonts, clean leftovers |
| You want a flat one credit per page | You want to choose the AI model or add a glossary |
| You want one button, not an editor | You want colorized pages now; RuneManga's colorizer is coming soon |

Sources: [Torii product page](https://toriitranslate.com/) · [pricing](https://toriitranslate.com/pricing) · [privacy policy](https://toriitranslate.com/privacy) · [updates](https://toriitranslate.com/updates)

## Ichigo Reader alternative: RuneManga vs Ichigo Reader

Ichigo Reader is a reader-first subscription service. Its pages describe an upload page that takes PDFs, ZIPs and images; a Chrome extension (listed as Manga Translator) that works on manga sites through its Unlocker or Panel Tool; apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android; Japanese, Chinese and Korean as sources with 18 target languages in the upload page's menu; and a choice of AI models, one of them on the free tier. The App Store lists Tier 1 at $8.99 and Tier 2 at $24.99, with a free trial that needs no registration. Its policy says uploaded images are kept "only as long as needed to provide and improve the Service", with no fixed period.

**RuneManga** reads 11 source languages and translates into 89, erases the original lettering and fills in the background, and deletes pages about an hour after the job. Its extension is on the Chrome Web Store and Edge Add-ons; it has no mobile app, but the WebApp works in mobile browsers.

| Pick RuneManga when... | Pick Ichigo Reader when... |
|---|---|
| You want a free plan rather than a trial | You want an app on iPhone, iPad, Mac or Android |
| You want the old lettering erased and a re-lettered PNG or ZIP to keep | You want to choose the AI model that translates |
| You want a stated retention time | You want to try without registering |

Sources: [Ichigo Reader upload page](https://ichigoreader.com/upload) · [Manga Translator on the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/lepcfgkehgeiblekejomdmdklmjdmflp) · [IchigoReader on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ichigoreader-%E6%BC%AB%E7%95%AB%E7%BF%BB%E8%AD%AF/id1641432177) · [privacy policy and terms](https://ichigo.moe/TOS.txt)

## manga-image-translator alternative: RuneManga vs manga-image-translator

manga-image-translator is a widely used open-source pipeline (GPL-3.0). You run it yourself, as a local web UI and an API; the README marks its online demo as no longer working. It is mainly built for Japanese as the source, with Chinese, English, Korean and others, and about two dozen target language codes. Inpainting models such as LaMa remove the text and a renderer sets the translation. Translators are your choice: DeepL, ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Papago and others with your own keys, or offline models such as Sugoi, NLLB and M2M100. It has an optional colorizer. It can run on a CPU, and a CUDA graphics card makes it much faster.

**RuneManga runs the same broad kind of pipeline as a hosted service**: nothing to install, fewer options. It is not built on manga-image-translator; it runs its own pipeline and keeps a license record for every model it uses.

| Pick RuneManga when... | Pick manga-image-translator when... |
|---|---|
| You do not want to install Python, PyTorch or a large Docker image | You want full control over the detector, inpainter, renderer and translator |
| You have no CUDA graphics card, or you are on a phone | You want everything offline on your own hardware |
| You want a working default with no settings to tune | You want to script batch jobs or build on its API |

Sources: [manga-image-translator on GitHub](https://github.com/zyddnys/manga-image-translator) · [Cotrans userscript on Greasy Fork](https://greasyfork.org/scripts/437569)

## Google Lens for manga: RuneManga vs Google Lens

Google Lens is free, built into the Google app and into Chrome's right-click menu on a computer, and covers more than 100 languages according to Google. It is a general-purpose image translator, for menus and signs as much as comics: there is no chapter upload, you translate one image at a time, and how the translation is shown depends on the device. The image is sent to Google and handled under Google's privacy policy.

**RuneManga** treats the page as a page: it finds each speech bubble, erases the old lettering, fills in the background and sets the translation inside the bubble, for a whole chapter in page order. Only the text reaches the translation engine, not the image.

| Pick RuneManga when... | Pick Google Lens when... |
|---|---|
| You want to read a whole chapter, not check one line | You want a quick idea of what one sign, note or bubble says |
| You want the translation set inside the bubbles | You want to point a phone camera at a printed page |
| You want to download the pages as PNGs or a ZIP | You need a language RuneManga does not offer |

Sources: [Google Lens product page](https://search.google/ways-to-search/lens/) · [Google Search Help: translate text with Lens](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/14516623) · [TechCrunch: Google Lens comes to Chrome (2022)](https://techcrunch.com/2022/05/24/google-lens-comes-to-chrome-for-searching-using-images-from-webpages/)

## BallonsTranslator alternative: RuneManga vs BallonsTranslator

BallonsTranslator is a free, open-source (GPL-3.0) desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux. Its README describes text recognition for Japanese, English and Chinese; inpainting (AOT, PatchMatch, LaMa); a what-you-see-is-what-you-get text editor with fonts, presets and effects; and a long list of translators: Google, DeepL, Papago, Sugoi (offline, Japanese to English), Sakura and other offline models, or any OpenAI-compatible model. It runs on your computer, and text leaves it only if you pick an online translator.

**RuneManga** is a hosted, one-button translator for reading: nothing to install and nothing to edit. The result is a normal PNG, so you can still retouch it in any image editor.

| Pick RuneManga when... | Pick BallonsTranslator when... |
|---|---|
| You want to read, not edit: add pages, press one button, download | You are translating your own comic or a licensed project and want to fix every line by hand |
| You do not want to install Python or set up a graphics card | You want to work offline with local models |
| You are on a phone or a computer you cannot install software on | You want to export the text to a Word document |

Sources: [BallonsTranslator on GitHub](https://github.com/dmMaze/BallonsTranslator)

## Which tool should I use? Straight answers

- **"I read a series on a website every day."** Try the RuneManga extension's Auto mode. If you need Safari or a mobile app, look at Torii or Ichigo Reader.
- **"I have a folder of pages and want to start for free."** The RuneManga WebApp: 30 free credits at sign-up, no card.
- **"I only need to understand one bubble."** Google Lens.
- **"I want to fix every line and font myself."** BallonsTranslator, or Torii's editor.
- **"I want everything offline, on my own hardware."** manga-image-translator or BallonsTranslator.
- **"I want colorized pages today."** Torii or manga-image-translator. RuneManga's colorizer is coming soon: we're working on it now.
