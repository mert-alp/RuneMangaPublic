# Every feature, and which plan gives what

**[← Back to the README](../README.md)** · **[Pricing on runemanga.com](https://runemanga.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=runemanga-public#pricing)** · **[Changelog](https://runemanga.com/changelog)**

The short version of RuneManga's pricing: **every plan gets the same pipeline and the same translator.** Free is not a cut-down engine. Every account, Free included, translates with RuneLingua, RuneManga's own AI translator for manga and manhwa dialogue, in the WebApp and in the browser extension, into every supported language. What the paid plans change is how many credits you get, how many pages fit in one job and how many pages are translated at the same time.

- **Free**: $0. 30 credits once, at sign-up, no card
- **Supporter**: $2.99 / month, 750 credits a month
- **Pro**: $4.99 / month, 2,000 credits a month
- **Ultimate**: $9.99 / month, 5,000 credits a month

Prices are in USD. The current numbers are always at **[runemanga.com/#pricing](https://runemanga.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=runemanga-public#pricing)**.

## How credits work

| Rule | What it means |
|---|---|
| **1 credit = 1 translated page** | The same in the WebApp and in the extension, on every plan |
| **Pages that fail cost nothing** | Pages that fail, or that are already in your language, cost nothing |
| **Free credits** | 30 once, when you create your account. They never expire |
| **Plan credits** | Granted on each renewal date. Unused ones do not carry over to the next period |
| **Pack credits** | Bought once, never expire, open to every account including Free |
| **Spending order** | Your plan's credits are always used first, then pack credits |
| **Running out** | Your plan stays, and its credits come back on the next renewal. Or buy a pack |
| **Upgrading** | The extra credits for the current period are added when the upgrade is paid |
| **Cancelling** | A cancelled plan keeps working until the end of the period you paid for, then you are back on Free |

Payments, tax and invoices are handled by **Lemon Squeezy**. Card details never reach RuneManga. Change or cancel your plan any time from *Manage subscription* on your account page; the [refund policy](https://runemanga.com/refunds) covers the rest.

**Invite friends.** Your account page has an invite link, and friends who join and translate earn you credits; more when they take a paid plan. The exact numbers are on the account page.

## The translation pipeline

The same on every plan, in the extension and in the WebApp.

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Find the text** | The speech bubbles on the page are located |
| **Read it** | Text recognition turns the lettering into characters. Japanese uses a reader built for manga; Korean and Chinese are supported and still improving |
| **Detect the language** | The original language is detected automatically, among 11 source languages. There is nothing to set |
| **Translate** | RuneLingua writes the translation. A few target languages it does not write well yet, and pages translated while it is briefly unavailable, go to Google Translate. Only the text goes to the translator, never the image |
| **Erase** | The original lettering is removed and the background underneath filled in, instead of a white box |
| **Typeset** | The translation is set in the same bubble at the largest size that fits. Lines wrap on word boundaries, and a long word is hyphenated rather than shrinking the text too far |
| **Keep the colours** | On coloured manhwa and webtoons, only the lettering changes |

## Browser extension features

Available in Chrome, Edge, Opera, Brave, Vivaldi, Arc and other Chromium-based browsers. A Firefox version is in review. Every feature below works on every plan; each translated page uses one credit.

### Translating on a reader site

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| **RuneManga button on each page** | Rest the pointer on a page and its button appears in a corner; click it to translate that page. Rest on it to open the page's bar |
| **Auto-translate as you scroll** | Turn on Auto and the pages near you translate as they scroll in, from about half a screen behind to about two screens ahead, nearest first. Remembered per site, so the next visit translates by itself |
| **Show original** | Switch a translated page back, or press and hold it to peek at the original while you hold. *Show originals* in the popup flips the whole tab |
| **Translate an area** | Drag a box over part of the page and only that part is translated, shown on top of it. For readers that draw pages in a protected canvas, and for a single panel |
| **Repeat last area** | Translate the same box again, from the right-click menu |
| **Screenshot an area** | Take a screenshot of a part of the page, from the right-click menu |
| **Translate image** | Right-click any image on a switched-on site and translate it |
| **Download translated image** | Save a translated page as a file, from the right-click menu or the page's bar |
| **Pause and resume** | Stop new pages from being translated, then carry on from where you are |
| **Automatic retries** | When the service is busy, a page tries again by itself; click it to try at once |
| **Progress line** | The popup shows how many pages on the tab are done, failed or still loading |
| **Smallest image to translate** | Only images at least this size are translated by themselves, so icons, ads and thumbnails do not use credits. Smaller images still get a button you can click |
| **Enable on every site** | RuneManga's button shows on reader sites everywhere, without switching each site on first. Nothing translates by itself until you turn Auto on |
| **Keyboard shortcuts** | Turn translation on or off for the site, translate an area, and show originals or translations on the tab. Rebind them in your browser's shortcut settings |

### Translation choices

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Translate into** | Pick the language you read, from the 89 targets. The original language is detected by itself |
| **Model** | RuneLingua is the default on every plan. With the next extension update you can pick Google Translate to translate in your browser instead |
| **Your own AI key** | Coming with the next extension update. Connect a key for OpenRouter, Google, OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, xAI or any OpenAI-compatible service (Mistral, Groq, a local Ollama or LM Studio and more) and translate with any model it offers. The key stays in your browser, for this account, and goes only to that provider. The provider bills you for its use; pages still use your RuneManga credits. If the key fails, that page falls back to Google Translate and the popup tells you why |
| **Reasoning level** | With your own AI key (next extension update), choose how long the model thinks |

### History and saving

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| **History** | Your last translated pages, kept in this browser only and never uploaded, with their originals. View, switch between original and translation, open full size, download or delete |
| **Auto-load previous translations** | When a page shows an image you already translated in this browser with the same settings, its translation appears at once, without using a credit |
| **Auto-delete** | Clear History after 1 day, 1 week or 1 month, or never |
| **Auto-save** | Save each translated page into a folder under Downloads |

### Account and support

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Sign in** | Google, Discord or email. The popup shows your plan and credits |
| **Report a problem** | Bug or feature request, the part of the extension it is about, optional screenshots and a capture of the page, optional diagnostics with every address cut to its site name |
| **Appearance** | Light or dark theme, and which top corner of each page the button sits in |
| **Interface languages** | 13: English, Español, Português, Français, Русский, 中文, Türkçe, Deutsch, Bahasa Indonesia, 한국어, العربية, 日本語 and ไทย |

## WebApp features

Works in any modern browser on a phone, tablet or computer, with nothing to install. It needs a free sign-in.

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Add pages** | Choose files, choose a folder (subfolders included), drag and drop, add a ZIP, or paste an image with Ctrl+V |
| **Formats** | PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF and AVIF, or a ZIP of them. A CBZ works after renaming it to `.zip` |
| **ZIPs opened in your browser** | Nothing is uploaded until you press the button. Only images are kept, folders inside are flattened and hidden files skipped |
| **Natural page order** | Files are sorted by name with numbers in order, so `2.png` comes before `10.png`. Thumbnails show the order before you start |
| **Page-by-page progress** | Follow each page as it is done |
| **Original and Result** | Flip or slide between the source page and the translation |
| **Workspace** | A bigger view with zoom, fit width, fit height and actual size |
| **Download** | One page as a PNG, or the whole chapter as one ZIP, numbered in order |
| **Translation history** | Your earlier translations kept in this browser only, never uploaded, with auto-delete after 1 day, 1 week or 1 month, or never |
| **Try a sample page** | See the pipeline on a page made for RuneManga before you upload your own |
| **Site languages** | The whole site in 13 languages, with guides, comparisons and a glossary in each |

## Extension or WebApp: which one to use

| | **Extension** | **WebApp** |
|---|---|---|
| Best for | Reading online, page by page, on the site you already use | Pages you have as files, a folder or a ZIP |
| Install | From the Chrome Web Store or Edge Add-ons | Nothing to install |
| Devices | Desktop Chromium browsers (Firefox in review) | Any browser, phones and tablets included |
| What you keep | Pages in History, downloads and Auto-save | PNGs and a ZIP to download within the hour |
| Readers with protected pages | Translate an area | Use pages you have as files |
| Credits | 1 per page | 1 per page |

## Not available yet

Said plainly, so nobody pays for something that is not there.

- **Colourising.** Colorizer: coming soon. We're working on it now, and it is not included in any plan yet. Translate works on black-and-white and coloured pages today.
- **Text outside speech bubbles.** Narration boxes, captions and loose text on the art are not translated yet. It is the next big piece being worked on.
- **Sound effects.** Left as drawn, on purpose. An option may come later.
- **Editing the result.** There is no way to edit the translated wording on the site yet. The result is a normal PNG you can retouch in any image editor.
- **PDF upload.** Convert a PDF to page images first: see [How to translate a manga PDF](https://runemanga.com/guides/translate-manga-pdf).
- **Safari.** There is no Safari version.
