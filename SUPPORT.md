# Getting help with RuneManga

This repository, RuneMangaPublic, is RuneManga's public information hub. RuneManga itself is a closed-source service: the extension comes from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/runemanga/cmclohfalopabbnmeoocelikbibbpppn) or [Edge Add-ons](https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/gmhdmcggedcjfmijpkladmmimfahnhjm), and the WebApp runs at **[runemanga.com](https://runemanga.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=runemanga-public)**. Most questions are answered fastest by the docs here or the guides on the site.

## Where to go

**1. [docs/troubleshooting.md](docs/troubleshooting.md)**: the common problems (text left untranslated, tiny text, the extension doing nothing on a site, refused uploads) and what actually fixes them.

**2. The guides** at **[runemanga.com/guides](https://runemanga.com/guides)**: step-by-step walkthroughs for the WebApp and the extension, plus write-ups on image quality, missed text, sound effects and privacy.

**3. The FAQ** at **[runemanga.com/faq](https://runemanga.com/faq)**, and the long-form one in **[docs/faq.md](docs/faq.md)**.

**4. Report a problem from the extension.** Open the RuneManga popup and choose *Report a problem*. Pick Bug or Feature request and the part of the extension it is about, describe it, and attach a screenshot or a capture of the page if it helps. Signed in, the report is linked to your account; signed out, leave an email address if you want an answer.

**5. Support on the website.** Signed in, the Support page at [runemanga.com/support](https://runemanga.com/support) files a bug report or feature request and keeps it as a conversation: we answer on the report's page, so check back there for replies.

**6. [Discord](https://discord.gg/TRwM9zgJPG)**: the community. Ask questions, share results, send a page that came out wrong, and see what is coming next.

**7. Email** **[support@runemanga.com](mailto:support@runemanga.com)**: for anything about your account, payments or your data.

Short demos and a tutorial are on **[YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@rrunemanga)**.

## What to include in a report

- **Where it happened:** the WebApp or the extension, and for the extension the site's name (not the full address)
- **The language pair**, for example Korean to English
- **What kind of text** was wrong: a line inside a bubble, text outside a bubble, a sound effect
- What you expected, and what happened instead
- A screenshot, if it is something you can see

Please only send pages you have the right to share.

## Billing and refunds

Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy. You can change or cancel your plan at any time from *Manage subscription* on your account page. The rules for cancelling and refunds are in the [refund policy](https://runemanga.com/refunds); for anything else, write to support@runemanga.com.

## Security

If you believe you have found a security issue, please report it **privately** by email to support@runemanga.com or by a direct message on [Discord](https://discord.gg/TRwM9zgJPG), not in a public channel. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
