## What it does

RuneManga reads the text on a comic page, cleans the speech bubbles and sets the translation back in, so the page still
looks like a page instead of a screenshot with subtitles on top.

<p align="center">
  <img src="assets/before-after.png" alt="The same manhwa page in Korean and translated into English by RuneManga" width="90%">
</p>

1. **Find the text.** It detects the speech bubbles and reads the original: Japanese, Korean, Chinese, English and more,
   or it detects the language by itself.
2. **Clean the bubbles.** The original lettering is removed and the art underneath is kept.
3. **Set your language.** The translation is fitted back into each bubble.

## Two ways to use it

### Browser extension

<img src="assets/extension-auto.gif" alt="Pages translate as you scroll with Auto on" width="360" align="right">

- **Auto mode:** turn it on and pages translate as you scroll, on the site you are reading.
- **A button on every page** to translate it, and one click to **show the original** again (or press and hold to peek).
- **Translate an area:** right-click, drag a box, and only that part is translated. It also works on readers that hide
  their images.
- **History:** the pages you translated are kept in your browser, with their originals.

Works in Chrome, Edge, Opera, Brave, Vivaldi, Arc and other Chromium browsers. A Firefox version is in review.

[Add to Chrome](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/runemanga/cmclohfalopabbnmeoocelikbibbpppn) ·
[Get it for Edge](https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/gmhdmcggedcjfmijpkladmmimfahnhjm)

<br clear="right">

### WebApp

No install: open [runemanga.com](https://runemanga.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=runemanga-public),
drop images, a folder or a ZIP, pick a language, and download every translated page back in one ZIP.

## Features

| | |
|---|---|
| **89 target languages** | From English, Spanish and Turkish to Indonesian, Vietnamese and many more. Arabic and Thai output is still experimental. |
| **Any direction** | Not only Japanese and Korean into English: English comics into your language work too. |
| **RuneLingua** | Our own AI translator, made for manga and manhwa dialogue. Every plan uses it, the free one included. |
| **Pay per page** | 1 credit = 1 translated page. A free account gets 30 credits, no card needed. Pages that fail cost nothing. |
| **13 site languages** | The website and the extension speak English, Español, Português, Français, Русский, 中文, Türkçe, Deutsch, Bahasa Indonesia, 한국어, العربية, 日本語 and ไทย. |

Plans and credit packs: [runemanga.com/#pricing](https://runemanga.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=runemanga-public#pricing)

## Good to know

- **Speech bubbles first.** Sound effects are left as drawn for now.
- **No colouring yet.** A colorizer is in development and is not offered until it works well.
- **Bring your own pages.** RuneManga translates the pages you open or upload; it is not a manga library.
